Health Guide

Complete Health Support

Before and after donation care, eligibility, and safety guidelines.

🍎

Before Donation

Prepare your body for a safe, successful donation.

  • Drink 500ml water 30 min before
  • Eat a light iron-rich meal
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours
  • Get 7–8 hours of sleep
  • Avoid fatty foods before test
  • Bring valid photo ID
✅

Eligibility Criteria

Basic requirements to donate blood safely.

  • Age: 18–65 years
  • Weight: minimum 50 kg
  • Hemoglobin: ≥12.5 g/dL
  • No fever or infection in 2 weeks
  • No tattoo/piercing in 6 months
  • Gap of 90 days since last donation
💊

After Donation Care

Recovery tips to feel great after giving blood.

  • Rest 10–15 mins at the centre
  • Drink extra fluids for 24 hrs
  • Eat iron-rich foods (spinach, meat)
  • Avoid heavy exercise for 12 hrs
  • Keep bandage on for 4 hours
  • Contact us if you feel unwell
⚠️

Who Should Not Donate

Temporary or permanent deferral conditions.

  • Pregnant or breastfeeding mothers
  • Recent surgery (past 6 months)
  • Active cancer treatment
  • HIV, Hepatitis B/C positive
  • Malaria in the past 3 months
  • On blood-thinning medication
